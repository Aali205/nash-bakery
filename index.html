№ 02 — Our Story

Born in Damascus.
Baked with heart.

What started in 2018 as a passion for craft baking grew into something bigger than we imagined — a belief that everyday baking can be artisanal, soulful and deeply personal.

From cinnamon rolls glazed and served warm at midnight, to cookies that carry the warmth of rose petals and pistachios — every item is crafted with care and an obsession for flavor.

№ 03 — A cookie born from a craving

The Damascene.

It started with a craving our founder, Dania Ayoubi, simply couldn't satisfy. She searched everywhere, found nothing — so she started testing in her own kitchen, batch by batch.

NASH had gathered flavors from around the world, but Syria deserved its own place on the menu. Damascus — city of the Damascene rose — gave the cookie its name and its soul.

  • White Chocolate
  • Aleppo Pistachio
  • Damascene Rose

№ 05 — From our oven

The English Cake.

01The batter

Butter, sugar, eggs and flour, creamed together and poured into the loaf tin.

02Into the oven

180°, low and slow, until it rises and cracks down the middle, the mark of a proper English cake.

03Fresh out

A golden crust, a soft crumb, and a smell that fills the whole bakery.

04The chocolate

Warm chocolate, poured slowly and left to drip down the sides.

05Finished

Crushed pistachio and Damascene rose on top. Now it is ready.
